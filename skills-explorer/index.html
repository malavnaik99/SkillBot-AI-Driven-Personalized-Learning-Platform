SkillBot skills explorer

Type a job title and see the skills that the most similar real job ads, and their job area, ask for. It matches job titles by meaning, so "data wrangler" can still find data analyst ads. Runs in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere. Part of Malav Naik's MSc project, SkillBot.

Data: job-skill-set by Batuhan Mutlu, from LinkedIn Job Postings by Arsh Koneru (both CC BY-SA 4.0). The skill lists were extracted by software, not people, and the data is a small sample of about 1,100 ads in a few job areas.
